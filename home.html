Japan (日本)

Japan is an island nation in East Asia known for seamlessly blending ancient traditions with ultra-modern technology. Located in the northwest Pacific Ocean, it features a volcanic landscape dominated by mountains, including the iconic Mount Fuji. As a global economic powerhouse, Japan is a leader in automotive manufacturing, electronics, and robotics. It is equally famous worldwide for its rich cultural exports, including sushi, anime, martial arts, and cherry blossoms. Its capital, Tokyo, stands as one of the most populous and technologically advanced metropolitan areas in the world.
